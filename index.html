Steps to Protect Yourself

Start with Small Amounts: If you decide to try GMGN.AI, start with a small deposit that you are willing to lose, as crypto trading is inherently risky.

Use a Hardware Wallet: Consider using a hardware wallet for storing your assets, which is much safer than leaving them in a platform wallet.
